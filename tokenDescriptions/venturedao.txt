VentureDAO is a decentralized autonomous organization pooling capital to invest in early-stage Web3 projects. With CDP, all profits or exit proceeds (e.g., token sales, buybacks) are redistributed automatically to VDAO token holders based on governance-approved allocation rules. This turns VDAO into an on-chain VC fund with transparent, non-custodial, and fair capital return paths—lowering barriers to decentralized wealth creation. VDAO organization is operating in crypto area and is obliged to share its proceeds with stake holders by legal contract. (check icons)
