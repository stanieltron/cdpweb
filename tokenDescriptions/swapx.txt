SwapX is a new decentralized exchange looking to attract experienced DeFi users. Instead of a random airdrop, the team uses the Compound Distribution Platform to target holders of the Uniswap (UNI) token — likely DEX users. By connecting SWPX to CDP, the project automatically distributes a portion of its launch supply to UNI holders, rewarding them proportionally without requiring them to claim or interact manually. This creates instant exposure to an ideal user base and builds goodwill through a relevant, trustless airdrop.
